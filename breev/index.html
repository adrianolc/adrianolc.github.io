What is B R E E V?

B R E E V is a minimalist breathing companion designed to help you slow down and reconnect with yourself. With clean visuals, smooth breathing animations, and calming rhythm guides, the app creates a peaceful space to release stress and regain focus in just one minute.

Stress builds up quietly — but so does calm. A few intentional breaths can reset your mind, lower tension, and boost clarity.

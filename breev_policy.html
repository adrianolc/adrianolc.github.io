Effective Date: November 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the BREEV mobile application. This policy describes how we handle the limited, non-personal data collected by the Service and confirms our commitment to your privacy.

1. Information We DO NOT Collect

We do not require or collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from our users. This includes, but is not limited to, your name, email address, physical address, phone number, date of birth, or any sensitive personal data.

Our application is designed to function entirely without the need for user accounts or personal input.

2. Non-Personal Usage Data

To help us understand how people use our Service, improve performance, and fix bugs, we automatically collect certain limited non-personal data related to your use of the app. This usage data cannot be used to identify you personally.

This type of data may include:

  • Device information (e.g., model, operating system version).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., features accessed, frequency of use).
  • Crash reports and anonymous performance data.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party tools to assist with performance analysis and application infrastructure. These services process only the non-personal usage data described above, strictly according to their own privacy policies.

The third-party providers we use are:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to understand app usage trends and patterns.
  • Firebase (Google): Used for backend services, crash reporting, and general application performance monitoring.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers to understand their data handling practices.

4. Security

Since we do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), the risk associated with data breaches is minimized.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected non-personal data from a child under the age of 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information. As we do not collect PII from any users, this section primarily serves as a legal notice.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
